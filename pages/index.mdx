## What is <EnvText envKey="projectName"/> Chain?

Xterio Chain, a true leader in Gaming Rollup, is dedicated to fulfilling the needs of billions of Web3 game players worldwide. With a steadfast promise to provide the utmost efficiency, security, and customization options, Xterio Chain enables seamless on-chain transactions within its ecosystem.

Xterio Chain is an indispensable member of Optimism community, leveraging the powerful OPSTACK technology and fortified by the Ethereum Mainnet and BNB Chain as its robust Layer1 security layers.

To enhance security and establish a more transparent and decentralized network, Xterio Chain has implemented Eigenlayer’s restaking mechanism. The team at Xterio Chain is proud to be among the pioneers of Rollups offering proactive verification services (AVS) post Eigenlayer’s mainnet launch and is pleased to announce that it is the first Gaming Rollup providing AVS. We believe that this development will have a significant impact in ensuring the safety and security of our network.
